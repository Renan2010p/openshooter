**OpenShooter 1.1 — Winter Warfare** is the Christmas edition of the free and
open-source LAN shooter by **RL PROJECTS**. The maps are covered in snow and
decorated for the holidays, every match now earns experience, and the main
menu is a brand-new lobby.

## Downloads

| Platform | File | How to run |
|---|---|---|
| **Windows** (64-bit) | `OpenShooter-1.1.0-windows-x86_64.zip` | Extract the zip and open `OpenShooter.exe`. |
| **Linux** (x86_64) | `OpenShooter-1.1.0-linux-x86_64.tar.gz` | Extract and run `./OpenShooter.x86_64`. |
| **macOS** (Intel + Apple Silicon) | `OpenShooter-1.1.0-macos-universal.zip` | Extract, then right-click `OpenShooter.app` → **Open** the first time. |
| **Android** (arm64 + armv7) | `OpenShooter-1.1.0-android.apk` | Copy to the phone and install (allow *Install unknown apps*). |

Keep the native library (`.dll` / `.so`) next to the executable on Windows and
Linux. `SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the checksums of every file.

## Highlights

### Christmas edition

- **Snow everywhere**: snow-covered ground and falling snow on Neighborhood,
  Harbor and Arctic Base. Desert Village stays sunny.
- **Holiday decorations** on every map: snowmen, Christmas trees with
  ornaments, a big tree with gifts near the middle of the map, wrapped gifts
  and colored string lights on the buildings. They are placed away from
  spawns, bot routes and streets, and are identical for every player in a
  match.
- **Santa hats** for every character by default (it is a Locker item).

### Levels and experience

- Every finished match gives **XP**: 50 for playing, 10 per elimination and
  **250 for a victory**.
- Levels get a little longer each time (400 XP to reach level 2, 500 more for
  level 3, and so on). The match-over screen shows what you earned and when
  you **level up**.
- Players coming from 1.0 start with the XP their saved statistics are worth,
  so nobody goes back to level 1.

### New Locker

- **39 free items** in seven categories: headwear, hair, face, top, legs,
  shoes and back (caps, helmets, cowboy hat, headphones, mohawk, long hair,
  sunglasses, beard, hoodie, tactical vest, suit, shorts, combat boots,
  backpack, cape and more), plus a new hair color.
- Items are shown as **3D thumbnails** in a scrolling grid; the camera zooms
  in on the part of the body you are dressing. Changes show on your character
  right away.
- Everyone in a match sees your look, and bots wear random outfits.
- The Santa hat is a Locker item, worn by default in this edition.

### New lobby

- Your character in the middle of the screen on a pedestal; drag to turn it.
- Top bar with your name, level and XP; holiday event banner; Locker and
  Statistics on the left.
- **Game mode card**: choose any of the five modes (Normal, Duel, Semi-Auto,
  Knife Fight, Gun Game) and the map before creating a server.
- Big **Play** button in the bottom-right corner. Server lists now show the
  mode each server is playing.

### Discord Rich Presence

- With the Discord app open on PC, your profile shows what you are doing in
  OpenShooter: in the lobby, or the mode, map, level, eliminations and players
  of your match, with the elapsed time and a download button.

### Under the hood

- The native map mesher was rewritten in **Zig** (it was C). It produces the
  same maps, removes more hidden faces (about 3% fewer vertices) and builds
  every platform, Android included, from one machine.

## Known notes

- The Store in the lobby is a preview and is not available yet.
- The builds are not signed by Microsoft or Apple. Windows SmartScreen may ask
  for confirmation (**More info → Run anyway**), and macOS needs
  **right-click → Open** the first time.
- 1.0 and 1.1 cannot play together (the holiday decorations change the map
  collision). Servers of the other version no longer appear in the LAN list,
  and joining one by IP shows a "Version mismatch" message.
